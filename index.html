Bonito offers end-to-end interior design, from space planning and design development to material selection, custom furniture, lighting guidance, décor, and complete on-site execution. Our teams manage every detail so your home comes together as one cohesive, thoughtfully finished space.

Full-home interiors are our core focus. In select cases, we also take on key spaces where the scope allows. In every project, we ensure the design feels complete and well considered.

Your dedicated designer guides you through every stage. From understanding how you live, to shaping concepts, presenting layouts and 3D visuals, selecting materials, and overseeing execution. The process is structured, clear, and designed to feel easy and reassuring.

Once designs are approved, most full-home projects move into a 90-day execution phase. Timelines may vary based on design complexity, material choices, and site conditions.
